Chunking:
    hunking means splitting large documents into smaller, manageable pieces (chunks) 
    before converting them into embeddings and storing them in a vector database.

    Types of Chunking:
        1.fixed size chunking - it based on character 
        2.token based - every chunk having # of tokens
        3.paragraph based - store chunk based on paragraph
