What is Transformation and Action?

Both are Types of Operation Performed on RDD, DataFrame and datasets

Transformation:
    All method which transform or change the data
    EX: .select() , .filter(), .group by(), .join(), withcolumn()
    Spark build DAG before the Transformation then it executes
Action:
    Executes the operation and Shows the output
    EX: .collect(), .save(), .show(), write(),overwrite()
    
What is  DAG (Directed Acyclic Graph)
    
    Definition: A graph structure with nodes (tasks) 
    and edges (dependencies) where no cycles exist.
    In Spark:
    When you apply transformations (map, filter, groupBy), Spark does not execute immediately.
    Instead, it builds a DAG of stages and tasks representing the logical execution plan.
    This DAG is later optimized and executed when an action (like collect(), count()) is triggered.
